1. Land Type Highlight Borders (Targeting/Casting)
When lands are highlighted on the board (e.g., when casting spells, deploying units, or selecting valid moves), their border glows with colors corresponding to their terrain types:

- Plains: Pure White (rgba(255, 255, 255, 1.0))
- Forest (Forrest): Vivid Emerald Green (rgba(34, 197, 94, 1.0))
- Mountain: Vivid Red (rgba(239, 68, 68, 1.0))
- Swamp: Vivid Purple (rgba(168, 85, 247, 1.0))
- Wizards Tower: Vivid Blue (rgba(59, 130, 246, 1.0))
- Default / Other: Vivid Aqua/Teal (rgba(0, 255, 204, 1.0))

2. Ownership Rings (Control)
When a land is claimed by a player, a solid border ring indicates control:

- Player (You): Blue (#0077ff)
- Sauron Bot: Red (#ff3333)

3. Selection & Hover Borders (Interactions)
- Active Selected Land: Bright Vivid Teal (#00ffcc)
- Active Selected Land Category: Semi-transparent Aqua (rgba(0, 255, 204, 0.8))
Hovered Land Hex: Semi-transparent White (rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8))
