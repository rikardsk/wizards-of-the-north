
# Spells

## Upgrade Land
- Card text: Upgrade target controlled territory level by 1.
- How it should work: When the player selects the spell card, highlight all lands that is currently owned by the player and is not the Wizards tower and has Level < 4. 
  The land must be of type Mountain, Plain, Swamp or Forrest. When the player clicks in a highlighted land then open the confirmation modal. Player confirms and pays 
  the mana cost after that change the tile to another tile of the same color with 1 Level higher and update the land tab. Also update the mana production. 
  Example the player clicks in a highlighted land that is Plain Level 2, then change the tile to Plain Level 3.
  
## Create Land
- Card text: Claim target unowned adjacent tile as controlled territory.
- How it should work: When the player selects the spell card, highlight all lands adjacent to a land that is currently owned by the player and the land itself is unowned.
  The land must be of type Mountain, Plain, Swamp or Forrest. When the player clicks in a highlighted land then open the confirmation modal. Player confirms and pays the 
  mana cost after that mark the land as owned and update the Land tab. Also update the mana production.
  
  ## Downgrade Land
- Card text: Downgrade target enemy territory level by 1.
- How it should work: When the player selects the spell card, highlight all lands that your opponent player owns except the Wizards tower and has Level > 1.
  The land must be of type Mountain, Plain, Swamp or Forrest. When the player clicks in a highlighted land then open the confirmation modal. Player confirms and pays the 
  mana cost after that change the tile to another tile of the same color with 1 Level lower.
  
  ## Destroy Land
- Card text:  Destroy target enemy territory, making it unowned.
- How it should work: When the player selects the spell card, highlight all lands that your opponent player owns except the Wizards tower.
  The land must be of type Mountain, Plain, Swamp or Forrest. When the player clicks in a highlighted land then open the confirmation modal. Player confirms and pays the 
  mana cost after that change the tile to be unowned and return any occupying creature to the players hand.
